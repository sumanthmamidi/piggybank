PiggyBank Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

In short: PiggyBank reads your bank and card SMS to track your spending, and keeps everything on your phone. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers, and the developer never receives your data. The only thing that ever leaves your phone is the optional backup to your own Google Drive, if you turn it on.

What PiggyBank is

PiggyBank is a personal spending tracker for Android. It finds payment messages from banks, cards and payment apps in your SMS, turns them into a list of payments, and helps you tag them into categories and budgets.

SMS messages

  • PiggyBank asks for permission to read SMS (to scan your inbox for past payments) and to receive SMS (to notice new payment messages as they arrive).
  • It uses SMS only to find financial transactions: the amount, date, account or card, and the payee.
  • Messages are processed on your phone. The payment messages it recognizes are stored in PiggyBank's private database on your phone. It doesn't send, forward or upload your SMS anywhere, and it never sends SMS.
  • It doesn't use SMS for advertising, profiling, credit scoring or anything other than showing you your own spending.

Location (optional, off by default)

  • If you turn on Save where I paid, PiggyBank notes your phone's approximate position when an in-person payment SMS arrives, so you can later see where a payment happened. Because payment SMS usually arrive while the app is closed, this uses background location: the location is read once when the payment SMS arrives, not continuously.
  • The location is stored only on your phone, next to that payment, and is included in your backups. "Open in Maps" hands that one spot to the maps app you choose.
  • You can turn this off at any time, and remove the saved place from any payment.

Notifications

PiggyBank can remind you to review the day's payments. Notifications are created on your phone.

Backups

  • On your phone: PiggyBank can save a backup file each night to the Documents/PiggyBank folder on your phone, so you can restore after reinstalling. That folder is on your phone's storage. You can delete these files at any time.
  • Google Drive (optional, off by default): if you connect Google Drive, PiggyBank also uploads the backup file to your own Google Drive, into a hidden app folder that only PiggyBank can open. It's sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) directly to Google. The developer can't see it. PiggyBank asks only for access to its own app folder, not to the rest of your Drive. Google's handling of your Drive is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

What PiggyBank doesn't do

  • No sign-up or account.
  • No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.
  • No servers: apart from the optional Google Drive backup, PiggyBank makes no network connections.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstalling PiggyBank deletes its database from your phone.
  • Backup files in Documents/PiggyBank stay until you delete them, so you can restore later.
  • To delete the Google Drive backup, choose "Turn off Drive" in PiggyBank, then in Google Drive on the web open Settings → Manage apps → PiggyBank → Delete hidden app data.

Children

PiggyBank is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly process their data.
